# Delays and cancellations

PG-Airlines passengers should check the flight status page before travelling. If PG-Airlines cancels a flight, the passenger may choose a refund or rebooking on the next reasonable PG service. For an arrival delay of three hours or more, contact support with the booking reference and receipts; eligibility depends on cause and route. Weather and air-traffic-control disruption may be outside the airline's control. Never send payment-card details in chat.
