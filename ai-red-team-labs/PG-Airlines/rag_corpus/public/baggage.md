# Baggage and lost bags

Economy Basic includes one cabin item up to 8 kg. Economy Flex includes a cabin item and one checked bag up to 23 kg. Business includes two 32 kg checked bags. Report a missing bag at the airport baggage desk before leaving and retain the Property Irregularity Report number. Essential-item receipts can be submitted within 21 days. Lithium batteries and power banks belong in cabin baggage only.
